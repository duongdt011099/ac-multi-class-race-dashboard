The dashboard

From entry list to chequered flag

No more spreadsheets. The whole championship lives in one desktop app that sits in the tray while you race.

Championships & seasons

Create championships, add seasons with a fixed number of rounds and build the calendar. Every round goes from not started to in progress to finished on its own. When the last round ends the season locks and the champion is crowned. Clone a season to reuse its calendar.

Not startedIn progressFinished

Automatic results

The in-game app exports every practice, qualifying and race result. The dashboard picks them up and updates standings and points. You don't touch a file.

One-click sessions

Start practice, qualifying or the race straight from the dashboard. The grid is ordered by class and qualifying position and the entry list is written for you.

Quick Race

A one-off race with no championship and no points. Pick a track, build a custom grid, drag cars into the start order and go. Your results are kept in a history.

Teams & drivers

Give each team its classes and each driver a car, skin, nationality, AI strength and aggression. Flag the human drivers, or add a whole batch from a preset.

Your classes, your colors

Name your classes, choose their colors and order, and keep several class settings with one set as the default.

Your points, your rules

Create your own point systems and pick one for each race. You decide who scores for finishing where.

Livery previews

See each car's livery from your own game install, so you always know who is who on the grid.

Updates built in

The app checks GitHub for new releases. Run the new installer and all your data stays.

Race control, inside the sim

The Multi_Class Lua app installs with the dashboard and switches on when you start a session from it. It runs the race the way an endurance race should run.

START

Rolling start

A formation lap behind the safety car, single file, then double file by class. The music is timed to end at the green and five green lights mark the start. Street circuits with hairpins get a safer double-file point.

SC

Safety car

A real car leads the field during the formation lap and keeps the AI from passing it.

FCY

Full course yellow

A stopped or crashed car brings out a yellow. Every car slows to the yellow speed with no closing up until the track is clear, then a countdown restarts the race.

BLUE

Blue flag lights

The track's own marshal panels show blue only near a car that is about to be lapped. They work in replays too.

LB

Class leaderboard

Overall and class positions with gaps, plus a compact view of your own class with fuel levels.

MAP

Live track map

The track outline with one dot per car in its class color, showing class or overall position.

AI

Smarter multi-class AI

Faster classes go around slower cars instead of queueing behind them. AI cars race side by side with care, and cars that get stuck are freed.

PIT

Crash guard

An AI car sent to the pits after a crash is repaired and gets one more chance. A second crash ends its race.

⏱

Timed races

Race from 5 minutes to 24 hours. Assetto Corsa only knows lap races, so the app runs the clock and ends the race on the leader's final lap.

How it works

Race weekend in six steps

  1. 01

    Create your championship

    Add a championship and a season with its number of rounds. Or clone a past season to reuse its calendar.

  2. 02

    Classes, teams & drivers

    Choose your class colors, then give each team its classes and add its drivers, one by one or from a preset.

  3. 03

    Build the calendar

    Add each round with a country, a point system and a race length in laps or time.

  4. 04

    Practice & qualifying

    Start each session from the dashboard. The results come back on their own and set the race grid.

  5. 05

    Lights out

    Rolling start, safety car, mixed-class traffic. Once you cross the line, standings and points are done.

  6. 06

    Crown a champion

    After the final round the season locks, trophies are handed out and the confetti flies.
