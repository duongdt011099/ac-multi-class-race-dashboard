Everything you need

From entry list to checkered flag

Stop juggling spreadsheets. The dashboard handles the whole championship lifecycle in one place.

🏆

Manage your series

Create championships and seasons, build the race calendar, and watch every round move from not started → in progress → finished. Clone a season to reuse its calendar.

🎨

Define your classes

Build class settings with your own names and colors, reorder them, and set a default. Hypercar, LMP2, GT3 — or anything you invent.

👥

Teams & drivers

Assign classes per team, set each driver's car, skin, AI strength and aggression, nationality — and flag the human drivers.

⚡

Auto-import results

Load practice, qualifying and race results. Standings and points are calculated for you — correct a result by simply importing it again.

🏁

Ready-to-load grids

Generate grid and race presets for Content Manager, ordered by class and qualifying position. Open and race.

🚗

Livery previews

See every car's livery right from your game install, so you always know who's who on the grid.

🧮

Your points, your rules

Create custom point systems and apply them per race. Who scores for finishing where is entirely up to you.

📊

Multi-class leaderboard

An in-game Lua app orders the leaderboard by class using your car tags, so you always see your real class position.

🔄

Stay up to date

The sidebar shows your version and checks for new releases. Updates keep all your data.

Inside the app

Built for race control

A clean, fast desktop experience in its own window — and it hides in the tray while you race.

How it works

Race weekend in six steps

1

Create your championship

Add a championship and a season. Clone a past season to reuse its calendar.

2

Set up classes, teams & drivers

Pick colors for each class, then assign teams and drivers — or batch-add from a preset file.

3

Build the calendar

Add each round with a country and a point system.

4

Practice & qualifying

Import the session results, then export the qualifying grid and race preset for Content Manager.

5

Race!

Rolling start, mixed-class traffic. Import the result afterwards — standings and points are done.

6

Crown a champion

Finish the final round: the season locks, trophies are awarded, and the confetti flies.
